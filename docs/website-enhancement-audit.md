## Local coverage

The directory now contains 15 original project guides: Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Kingwood, Richmond, Galveston, Magnolia, Cypress, Houston Heights, League City, Friendswood, Pearland, Webster, La Porte. Seven guides were added: Cypress, Houston Heights, League City, Friendswood, Pearland, Webster and La Porte. The existing eight guides were retained and connected to relevant dedicated service pages. Houston Heights was represented as a place rather than an independent city in structured coverage data.

Each new guide offered different preparation advice: cabinet inventory in Cypress, existing trim and ceiling details in Houston Heights, plumbing and support in League City, exposure and appliances in Friendswood, workspaces and door travel in Pearland, merchandising in Webster, and indoor/outdoor material planning in La Porte. Location guides did not claim that portfolio photographs depicted work installed in those cities. No service-by-city doorway-page matrix was created.

## On-page and technical SEO

Unique titles and descriptions were registered for all 20 services and seven new areas. Metadata gaps for the ten residential portfolio collections were corrected. All 61 public routes were included in the XML sitemap and existing static-rendering pipeline. The HTML sitemap updated automatically. Robots.txt was reviewed and already allowed indexing and advertised the correct sitemap. Existing noindex treatment for admin and missing routes was preserved.

Canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter metadata, visible and structured breadcrumbs, Service/LocalBusiness/Organization data, relevant service imagery and accessible image descriptions were supplied through the existing SEO components. Structured service data identified the studio as provider without inventing a street address, reviews or ratings. Related services, portfolio pages, materials guidance, local guides and the footer were connected with descriptive links. The excessive city list in the About narrative was replaced with useful local-guide links.

Existing responsive Netlify Image CDN delivery and lazy loading were reused, with eager loading reserved for the principal service image. No new dependencies, plugins, persistence changes or migrations were introduced. Responsive service layouts used two columns on wider screens and one on mobile.

## Verification

The SEO check rendered all 61 public routes and verified unique metadata, one H1 per route, canonical URLs, valid JSON-LD, internal page and anchor links, image-file existence, sitemap parity and missing-page handling. Service-area and language/navigation regression checks passed. The new services check verified all 20 service routes, original advice, required coverage locations, project connections, semantic emphasis, provider schema and honest photo labeling. Whitespace validation passed. Production builds were left to platform validation; live Core Web Vitals and search performance were not measured.

## Client material still needed

Exact matching project photographs were not identifiable among the provided assets for: Floating Shelves, Accent Walls, Custom Booths, Pocket Doors, Custom Bunk Beds, Custom Desks, Barn Doors, Custom Staircases, Floating Vanities, Custom Mantels. Those pages used accurately captioned related client photographs; no stock images or mislabeled completed installations were introduced. Wood Staining & Sealing used a finished tabletop reference; a before/after or application-process example would strengthen it. Commercial booth seating should have its own image rather than relying on the residential bench reference.

A public street address was not supplied and was not invented. If the studio has a customer-facing location, confirm the public address before adding it to business schema or map information. Confirm service-specific materials, hardware, typical schedules, warranties and any publishable project facts before adding those claims. No rankings, lead volumes, certifications or review totals were promised or fabricated. Google Business Profile ownership and Search Console access were not available in this repository, so external profile edits and sitemap submission were not performed.
