This document maps the implemented public pages to search intent. Priorities reflect commercial relevance and the available portfolio, not measured keyword volume, difficulty or live search rankings. ## Ownership and search intent

The homepage owns broad Houston custom woodworking and furniture-maker searches. The service directory helps visitors select a capability; each service page owns a narrower purchase-intent cluster. Project pages supply evidence and local guides answer location-specific planning questions. The Houston guide focuses on planning rather than repeating the homepage proposition.

Synonyms share a canonical owner: dining tables stay with live-edge tables; cabinet makers and closets stay with custom cabinets; bookshelves stay with built-ins. The existing scope and photographs support these consolidated pages. A separate dining-table or closet URL is not recommended unless distinct, verified work and sufficient original content justify it. Near-me searches are supported by real coverage, relevant content and contact details; no literal near-me doorway pages were created.

## Content and conversion rules

Use a useful title, one clear H1, original planning advice and relevant client photography on each service page. Emphasize important phrases with strong text where it helps scanning; bold styling is not a ranking guarantee. Keep image alternative text about the visible subject instead of appending lists of cities. Link related services by their actual subject and route visitors to project proof, the material guide and a quote. FAQs answer scope, cost factors and practical decisions without inventing prices, lead times or reviews.

The 14 requested communities are covered by existing distinct guides; Magnolia remains as an existing additional area. Do not create a service-by-city page matrix or invent offices in served communities. Keep portfolio locations unattributed unless confirmed by the client. LocalBusiness, Service and BreadcrumbList markup must match visible content and verified information. Service FAQ markup reflects visible answers but does not imply eligibility for Google FAQ rich results.

## Technical work applied

Titles, meta descriptions, service H1s and internal links were aligned with this ownership model. Project titles identify photo collections. Existing responsive Image CDN delivery, lazy loading, canonical URLs, mobile layouts and static sitemap generation were retained. Twenty-eight legacy photographs received descriptive filenames with permanent redirects for old URLs. The hero renders its first photo initially and loads other slide photos on activation. Responsive portfolio image sizes were aligned with their displayed widths. Prerendered HTML is hydrated instead of discarded, and reveal content is visible before JavaScript initializes.
